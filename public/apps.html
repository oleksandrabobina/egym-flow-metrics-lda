Incidents (MTTD & MTTR) Dashboard

ℹ️ About this report

This is the DORA Incidents (MTTD & MTTR) report for the Apps — the BMA Core Growth, BMA Engagement & Adoption, Trainer, Experience, Web, MMS-Connect, BMA, BMA-Core, XT engineering teams. In plain words, it answers two key questions: how fast do we notice an incident, and how fast do we recover from it. It is generated automatically from the same data feeds as the official DORA workbook, so the numbers match.

Abbreviations & Terminology

Term What it means
MTTD Mean Time To Detect — hours from when an incident's impact starts to when it is identified.
MTTR Mean Time To Restore — hours from when impact starts to when it is fixed.
Calendar Year Metrics and trends filtered strictly from 1 January to 31 December for the selected calendar year.
YTD Year To Date — aggregated metrics calculated from 1 January of the selected calendar year up to the latest recorded incident date.
Mean vs Median Mean = average; Median = the middle value. A big gap between them means one or two extreme incidents are pulling the average up.
